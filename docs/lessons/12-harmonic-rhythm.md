# 12: Harmonic rhythm and arrangement

**Status:** outlined
**Builds on:** 05, 06

## Goal
Understand how chord length, rhythm, bass lines and the split of harmony across layers
shape the emotion of the same progression.

## Key concepts
- **Harmonic rhythm:** how often chords change.
- **Anticipation (push):** a chord arriving an 8th or 16th before the bar line.
- **Bass line styles:** root notes, rolling offbeat, walking, pedal.
- **Layering:** splitting harmony across pad, arp, bass and lead.
- **Register and spacing:** low close voicings get muddy; spread them out.
- **Arrangement arc:** breakdown, build and drop with the same chords.

## Sections
1. **Harmonic rhythm:** one chord per 2 bars versus two chords per bar.
2. **Anticipations:** pushed chords for pop and EDM feel.
3. **Bass line styles:** the same chords under four bass patterns.
4. **Layering:** pad, arp and bass each playing the harmony differently
   (like the earlier trance player).
5. **Register and spacing:** muddy versus clear low voicings.
6. **The arrangement arc:** one progression through breakdown, build and drop.

## Main experiment
One progression arranged in three genres (trance, pop ballad, lo-fi), switching live.

## Quiz ideas
Ear: fast or slow harmonic rhythm; which layer carries the chord change.

## Components and theory
- **New:** progression builder with MIDI export; layer mixer.
- **Theory:** rhythm pattern definitions; MIDI file writer.
