# 11: Tension devices

**Status:** outlined
**Builds on:** 04, 05, 08, 10

## Goal
Know a toolbox of devices that add tension, surprise or lift to a progression, and hear
each one in context.

## Key concepts
- **Pedal point:** a sustained bass note under changing chords (tonic or dominant pedal).
- **Line cliché:** one voice moving chromatically inside a held chord (Am, Am(maj7), Am7, Am6).
- **Chromatic mediant:** major chords a third apart sharing one note (C–E, C–Ab).
- **Modulation:** changing key via a pivot chord or directly.
- **Truck-driver key change:** a direct shift up a semitone or whole tone.
- **Planing:** moving one chord shape in parallel.

## Sections
1. **Pedal points:** the same loop with and without a tonic pedal.
2. **Line clichés:** motion inside a single chord.
3. **Chromatic mediants:** the film-score "wow" move.
4. **Pivot-chord modulation:** a chord that belongs to both keys as the bridge.
5. **Direct key changes:** the final-chorus lift.
6. **Planing:** parallel chord shapes, from Debussy to house music.

## Main experiment
A 16-bar trance breakdown where each device can be toggled in, one at a time or stacked.

## Quiz ideas
Ear: which device is used; find a pivot chord between two keys.

## Components and theory
- **New:** device toggles on a fixed arrangement (possibly a simple section editor).
- **Theory:** `chromaticMediants(chord)`, `pivotChords(keyA, keyB)`, `transpose(progression, n)`.
